HOW TO PLAY

WASD move   Space jump (clears one level)   mouse aims

String up to three, then left click to cast at the cursor. Right click clears. Change the keys under CONTROLS.

Earth comes from somewhere. A punch needs a rock, a wall, or a body in front of you. Boulders are ripped from the ground and leave craters; spent ground turns to dark bedrock and gives nothing. Walls are ammo. Rubble is ammo.

Take the high ground. Earth thrown downhill flies with gravity; thrown uphill, against it. A pillar under your own feet is a firing platform, and a sinkhole under theirs takes it away.

Your feet must touch earth to move the ground. In the air or on steel you can still punch and catch rocks.

Your rival sees your signs above your head, and you see theirs. Cracks on the ground mean something is about to erupt there: move.

Fall off the mesa and you lose the round. First to three rounds wins. After 60 seconds the rim starts to crumble.

House rules (the host picks). Breath: every sign costs one breath and breath returns on its own, so both benders get the same number of casts and the duel is about which one and when; formed signs never fade, and you can see how much breath your rival has left. Simple scroll: 15 casts instead of 24.
